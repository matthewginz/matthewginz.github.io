10-Q Equity Research Agent

Summer 2025 – Present

A 6-step LLM pipeline (Python, Gemini API) that turns each company's 10-Q text and XBRL financials, pulled live from SEC EDGAR, into a Bullish/Neutral/Bearish call, deployed as a public Streamlit app. Backtested on 500+ S&P 500 filings (growing nightly) using point-in-time data only, against Wall Street consensus, momentum and always-bullish baselines, with binomial tests and a long-short portfolio. It matched Wall Street at picking outperformers (51% vs. 50%), with neither beating a coin flip, and its 54% raw hit rate trailed a 64% always-bullish baseline (p = 0.07).

Kaiser-Stats

Jul 2026 – Present

A stats engine that unifies 5 seasons (2022–2026, ~280+ games) of a local soccer league's inconsistent spreadsheet history into one per-player dataset. Weekly updates are automated: a scheduled routine uses the Gemini API to parse the league's game-report emails and add the weekend's games, then a separate stress tester independently validates the results. The public registration and stats site is used by a 110+ person league mailing list, with data-integrity safeguards (player identity resolution, plus-minus sanity checks) and a transparent power-ranking formula.
